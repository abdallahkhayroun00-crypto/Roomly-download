Roomly Privacy Policy

Updated 4 October 2026. Contact: roomlyapp.team@gmail.com.

What Roomly processes

Roomly uses Firebase Authentication for account identifiers, email/password sign-in and Google sign-in. Google sign-in provides your name, email and profile information. Firestore stores your profile, apartment membership, shared expenses and payment history, rent and bills, chores and completion history, and household needs and purchases. Optional saved IBANs are private profile data; an IBAN deliberately added to an expense is visible in that apartment's payment details.

Who can see shared information

Active apartment members can see shared records according to their roles. Roomly does not sell your information or use it for advertising. Google operates Firebase and Drive; their privacy terms also apply. Services communicate over encrypted HTTPS. No claim is made that shared records are end-to-end encrypted.

Receipts and Drive

Receipt selection uses the Android system file picker or camera application. Roomly requests the limited drive.file scope and stores receipts in your Google Drive Roomly Archive. The updated app keeps new receipts private to the uploading Google account. Roommates may see an attachment name and Drive reference in an expense but cannot open an owner-private file without separately authorized Drive access.

Earlier beta versions created anyone-with-link access. Updating the app does not automatically revoke every previously uploaded file. Review old receipt sharing in Google Drive and remove public access. For assistance, contact support without sending the receipt itself. Removing an attachment in Roomly does not delete the original Drive file. You control that file in Drive and can revoke Roomly's Google access in your Google account settings.

Notifications and devices

Personal reminders, notification history, preferences and pending schedules are stored on your phone. Notification permission is optional. When an enabled backend registers push tokens, Firebase uses those tokens for notification delivery. Remote push delivery is unavailable in the current Firestore-only beta. Signing out clears local reminder schedules and history.

Analytics, fonts and website

The updated Android app does not intentionally include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Earlier builds may contain Firebase Analytics. Recheck the actual release before making Play Console declarations. App fonts may be fetched through Google Fonts; this website also loads Google fonts on its landing page and checks public GitHub releases. Those services receive ordinary network request information such as IP address.

Retention and deletion

Account and apartment information is retained while needed to provide the service. Shared financial and completion history may need to remain for other roommates after departure. Existing beta records can retain historical names and identifiers; full anonymization is a release requirement still being completed. Personal profile and authentication deletion is supported by the trusted backend when enabled; the current beta also accepts verified manual requests.

You can start a deletion request from the account deletion page or by email. We verify ownership before acting. Tell us if you also want help removing older shared personal details. Never send passwords, OAuth tokens, full IBANs or receipt images. Drive files are controlled by your Google account and must be removed in Drive separately.

Your choices

You can choose whether to attach receipts, save an IBAN, link Google or enable reminders. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion of your information. Do not use real banking screenshots or other unnecessary sensitive information in the beta.
