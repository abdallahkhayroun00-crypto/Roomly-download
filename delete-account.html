Request deletion of your Roomly account

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the app. Email roomlyapp.team@gmail.com from the email used for your Roomly account, with the subject “Roomly account deletion request”. We will verify ownership before acting. Never send your password, invite code, bank details or receipt images.

In an app build with the trusted lifecycle backend enabled, use Settings → Delete account. The Firestore-only beta requires organizer assistance; the email request remains available.

What is removed or retained

Deletion requests cover your Firebase Authentication account, private Roomly profile and saved IBANs, notification-token associations and apartment membership. Shared expenses, payments and chore history may remain to preserve other roommates' records. Request removal or anonymization of personal details in those records as part of the same request; the automatic anonymization workflow is not yet release-verified.

Pending shared debts and administrator responsibilities require coordination so other roommates' records are not lost. Support will explain any retention and required transfer rather than merely disabling your account.

Your original receipt files remain in your Google Drive. Delete them there and revoke Roomly access from your Google account if desired. Earlier beta receipts may have public link sharing; remove those permissions in Drive.
