What is included in this beta?

Shared expenses, chores, bills, supplies and apartment preferences are available. Automatic notifications and reminders, account deletion, leaving or deleting an apartment, removing active members and deposit payment updates are not available yet. A LITTLE HELP

Before you move in.

Can I download Roomly now?

The Android private beta is ready for invited testers. Ask the organizer for the APK. Public releases, when published, will appear in the download section.

Are these actual app screenshots?

Yes. These are screenshots captured from an Android test build using a demo apartment, not illustrations. The interface shown is in English; the selector on this website changes the website language.

How do I join an apartment?

Create an account and verify your email. Your apartment admin can then invite you. Keep apartment invite codes private.

Will I receive reminders?

Automatic notifications and scheduled reminders are not available in this private beta.

How do I report a problem or delete my account?

Contact the organizer below. Account and apartment changes need organizer assistance during the private beta. Do not send passwords or apartment invite codes.

Your home. Your information.

This website does not ask for your app password, banking details or apartment invite code. It loads fonts from Google and checks public GitHub releases. Information added inside a shared apartment may be visible to its members according to their access. This overview does not replace a full privacy policy.
